How is my price decided?

WRINGG sorts the clothes and matches them to suitable machines from 5 to 10 kg. The ₹150 starting reference is for 5 kg machine capacity. Your service and load determine the final price; check it in the app before booking.

What is included?

Everyday laundry includes express service, pickup and return. Check the selected service and its inclusions in the app. Extra services, including ironing, are included only when the selected service says so. Special-care items are priced separately.

When will it be back?

Check the pickup and return timings for your address and selected service in the app before booking.

What about special-care items?

Price and suitability depend on the item, material, size and service. Check shoes, curtains, bags, carpets and other items in the app or ask WRINGG support.
