A FEW THINGS, UNFOLDED.

Good
questions.

Can I book a pickup in BTM?+

The WRINGG pilot starts in BTM Layout, Bengaluru. Book through the WRINGG customer app on Android or iPhone beta. Check your address, availability and pricing in the app.

Which app should I download?+

For pickups, use Google Play or the iPhone TestFlight beta. For home and delivery onboarding, use the WRINGG Partner app. Laundry businesses can use our commercial enquiry page.

What services can I ask for?+

Ask about Wash & Fold, Wash + Iron and Steam Iron. Confirm availability, care requirements, pricing and turnaround in the app or with our team before placing your order.

Do I need a laundry shop to become a partner?+

No. If you have a suitable home laundry setup, see the Home Partner steps. Businesses can use the commercial enquiry page.
