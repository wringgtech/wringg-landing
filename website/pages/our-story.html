HIS SIDE OF THE CITY

The city is new.
Missing home isn’t.

A festival evening. A room far from family. And a pile of laundry he keeps putting off.

Even a favourite shirt can feel unfamiliar.

HER SIDE OF THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Her machine has room.
Her family has plans.

She joins WRINGG as a home partner. With training and checks complete, her care becomes paid work nearby.

An earning opportunity, right where life happens.

WRINGG CONNECTS THE TWO

One booking.
A neighbourhood connection.

He books in the app. A WRINGG rider collects his clothes and brings them to a trained home partner nearby.

The bag travels. The care stays close.

THE PART THAT WASN’T IN THE ORDER

Clean clothes.
Something sweeter.

She remembers he is away for the festival. Alongside his fresh clothes, she adds homemade sweets and a little note.

“A little taste of home.
Happy Diwali.”

Her personal gesture, in this imagined story.

HIS DOOR, AGAIN

A fresh shirt.
A familiar feeling.

The rider returns his laundry. He opens the note—and for a moment, the city doesn’t feel so far from home.

“Thank you,
aunty.

It feels a little more like home here.

“Thank you, aunty.
It feels a little more like home here.”

A reply imagined for this story.

BACK AT HER HOME

Her care earns.
Her family dreams.

Paid work close to home. A little more room to plan. The everyday things that help a family move forward.

One bag. Two homes. More than laundry.

THAT’S THE NEIGHBOURHOOD WE’RE BUILDING.

A little closer.
A little more home.

For the person wearing the clothes.
For the person caring for them.
